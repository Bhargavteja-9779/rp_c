| 6.3 | "State the complexity, not only seconds on one machine." | MODERATE | Added to this review and summarised in 4.7: with n training spectra, p wavelengths, A latent variables and K groups, the group-wise CV costs K+1 PLS fits, O(K·n·p·A) — the same work as standard group-wise selection of A.
