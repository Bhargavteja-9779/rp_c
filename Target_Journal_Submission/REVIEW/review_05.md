The reviewer actively tried to invalidate the central claim ("calibrating on out-of-group errors restores coverage for new groups").

| # | Issue / attack | Severity | Action | Outcome | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | "Seasons may not be exchangeable; there may be a temporal trend." | MAJOR | Forward-chaining scenario (train on earlier seasons only) was in the protocol. | GC-D 0.877 vs SCP 0.783 on next seasons; claim holds without assuming random season order. | Addressed |
| 5.2 | "Test an unseen factor that is likely *not* exchangeable with training groups: a new cultivar." | MAJOR | New experiment: leave-one-cultivar-out (10 cultivars, 9 methods, seeds 0–2; experiments/run_cultivar.py). | All methods close to nominal (SCP 0.900, ASTM-R 0.907, GC-D 0.902; IS 4.71 vs 4.68). Reported in Section 4.6 and Table S15. The central claim is narrowed: GC-D helps when the held-out factor carries an *unrepresented* group effect. | Fixed (experiment + narrowed claim) |
