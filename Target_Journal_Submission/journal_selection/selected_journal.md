## Honest statement on the ≤ 72-day requirement

No reputable Q1 journal guarantees acceptance within 72 days. Among the 43 journals for which we could measure real submission-to-acceptance times,
only Analytica Chimica Acta (median 58.5 d) and Talanta (median 66 d) had journal-wide medians ≤ 72 days.
Talanta was preferred because (a) its 2025 JIF is higher (6.7 vs 6.1), and (b) for the specific paper type
(chemometrics/ML) its measured median is shorter (74.5 d vs 85 d). For this paper type, roughly half of accepted papers
were accepted within 72 days. The figure is a historical median, not a promise.

Backup journal: **Analytica Chimica Acta** (Elsevier, ISSN 0003-2670, 2025 JIF 6.1, Q1 Chemistry, Analytical).
